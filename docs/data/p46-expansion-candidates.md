## Concrete mismatches and remaining gaps

1. **Side labels are unreliable.** Every Michigan manifest's generic label says Recto. For 1480, `Section/Side` also says `163; Recto`, while `Publ./Side`, the cited table f.83v and TEI say Verso. For 1500 and 1520, the detailed section/publication fields correctly say Verso while the generic label remains Recto. Preserve these conflicts and require image/folio confirmation instead of copying one caption as truth.
2. **Terminal line alignment needs review.** Pages 1500 and 1520 each have one more TEI line-break element than the catalogue line range suggests. The source structure may explain this, but no visual count or automatic reconciliation was performed. The 1490/1500 catalogue records also include correction notes near terminal lines; do not use their edition readings as visible labels without adjudication.
3. **Damage and faintness are unmeasured.** Catalogue conservation notes describe right-edge cutting for 1470/1480/1500 and left-edge cutting for 1490/1510/1520. All six TEI files contain supplied and unclear text, plus abbreviations; several contain apparatus alternatives. Their exact markup counts are retained. Nothing here establishes that these are the faded training examples needed to match page 1440 or that restored lines are usable.
4. **The source URI is not always a fetchable URI.** NTVMR's metadata transcription entries contain a `localhost:8080` URI. The successful reads used the public `ntvmr.uni-muenster.de/community/vmr/api/transcript/get/` endpoint with the recorded docID/pageID. Preserve original metadata while resolving access through the documented public host; do not follow the localhost value on a user's computer.
5. **The Michigan portion stops here in this sequence.** NTVMR page 1530 is listed under the Chester Beatty shelf, not Michigan shelfID5439. Do not extend Michigan's PDM status by manuscript number or adjacent pageID. Earlier Michigan leaves can be investigated separately if more objects/conditions are needed.
6. **Volume and evaluation readiness remain open.** These three additional leaf pairs, plus the previously examined leaves, do not yet supply the planned eight physical leaves or 100/30/30 reviewed-line partitions. An evaluator must choose genuinely fresh acceptance material separately. This remains one Koine witness and adds no unseen-hand, unseen-witness or Classical Greek validation.
7. **The TEI header is witness-level, not page ownership.** Each response identifies P46 but lists both Chester Beatty and Michigan holdings. Choosing the first `msIdentifier` would wrongly assign these pages to Chester Beatty. Preserve both header records and resolve this candidate image's holding institution through NTVMR page-level shelfID5439 and the exact Michigan APIS item.
8. **Catalogue and capture identifiers are not interchangeable.** APIS3615–3620 in this report are Michigan image-manifest/processing identifiers. All six manifests link the same external catalogue identifier, `papyri.info/apis/michigan.apis.3620`. Preserve that recorded link separately; do not deduplicate six different surfaces into one image or substitute the catalogue number into a capture URL.

For the next **source-review** round, inspect 83r/v together first, then 84r/v and 85r/v. This order follows the existing leaf-82 pilot and keeps sibling surfaces together; it is not a training assignment or a judgment of image quality. Acquire only the authorized institutional derivatives, verify each visible incipit/explicit and folio against the source, place reviewed geometry, and adjudicate visible versus restored text and correction states before handing labels to training.
